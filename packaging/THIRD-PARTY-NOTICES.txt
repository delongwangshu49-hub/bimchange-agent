Distribution review / 分发复核
------------------------------

files. No technical restriction is added to prevent compatible library
replacement or debugging modifications. This fact alone does not discharge
all notice, source and installation-information obligations.

Generic GPL/LGPL texts, Python's runtime license and selected wheel metadata
are copied into validation packages. Complete exact-version Qt/Chromium and
native dependency attributions/corresponding-source materials must still be
assembled and checked against the FINAL binary file list. Do not claim a
matching public source tag exists before it is actually published.

https://doc.qt.io/qt-6.11/qtquick3d-index.html

modules through a tested dependency-minimization build or establish the

license or assume a commercial license has been purchased.
